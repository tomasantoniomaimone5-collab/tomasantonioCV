# tomasantonioCV
CV Website - Hypermedia Project (Part 1)

1. Project Analysis: User Profile (User Persona)

Name: Isaac Güell

Age: 23

Occupation: Talent Acquisition Specialist & Junior Recruiter at an indie / AA game development studio.

Goals: Identify promising junior game designers and developers with a strong technical mindset, high adaptability, practical problem-solving skills under pressure, and solid teamwork capabilities.

Frustrations:

Cluttered CV websites where contact information or fundamental educational background is hard to locate.

Difficulty quickly assessing a candidate's real-world work ethic, transferable technical skills, and language competencies.

Web pages with poor layout hierarchy or slow loading times that hinder evaluation efficiency.

Expectations: A clean, intuitive, single-page CV that instantly presents Tomàs Antonio's academic background (Grau en Disseny i Desenvolupament de Videojocs), practical technical work experience (electrician at Domènec Maimoné SL), core competencies, language proficiencies, and direct contact details without unnecessary friction.

2. Project Analysis: Information Architecture

The information architecture of this single-page CV project is structured to offer a smooth, logical, and effortless user experience through vertical scrolling. The layout guides recruiters step-by-step from an immediate visual introduction down to specific skill sets and contact information.

At the top, the Header / Hero Section displays Tomàs's profile portrait (tomas.png), my title (Student of Disseny i Desenvolupament de Videojocs in Girona), and an immediate call-to-action button ("Contact with me") that smooth-scrolls to the contact section. The content then flows into the About Me section, summarizing my academic background (transitioning from a Social Sciences Batxillerat into Game Design) and emphasizing my versatility, adaptability, discipline, and practical problem-solving mindset applied to interactive media.

Next, the Laboral Experience section details my  work as an electrician at Domènec Maimoné SL (June–August 2024 & June–August 2025) and in Revlon(July 2026-September 2026). It outlines key responsibilities including electrical installation, maintenance, repair, schematic interpretation, structured cabling layout, and working under high pressure in a coordinated team to meet safety standards.

Following experience, the Education section presents my academic journey in reverse chronological order:

Grau en Disseny i Desenvolupament de Videojocs (2025–2029)

Batxillerat Social at Serra de Miramar (2023–2025)

Dual American Diploma at Escola Claret Valls (2022–2025)

The page concludes with  two-column section for Habilities (teamwork & communication, rapid adaptability, troubleshooting technical issues under pressure, analytical thinking & organization) and Idioms (Native Catalan & Spanish, English B2–C1, French A2), terminating at the Contact Footer featuring my email, phone number, and location (Alcover and Girona).

3. Project Analysis: Visual Design

The visual design is built around clean readability, accessible content hierarchy, and a lightweight user interface optimized for rapid scanning by hiring managers. 

A neutral background color scheme is paired with dark body text to maintain optimal contrast ratios. Also there’s a header in blue to make more entusiasm in the name and the photo. Distinct headings (<h2>, <h3>) establish clear visual section boundaries, while generous line-heights and structured lists prevent visual clutter. The profile photo is centered in the header to establish immediate visual identity.

Strategic accent colors highlight key interactive elements, such as the "Contact with me" action button. The responsive layout organizes experience, education, skills, and languages into clean visual blocks. On larger desktop displays, skills and languages sit side-by-side to maximize screen real estate, stacking cleanly into a single vertical column on mobile screens.

5. Technologies and Implementation Decisions

HTML5 & CSS3: Structured using clean semantic HTML tags (<header>, <section>, <footer>, <ul>, <ol>) and a custom external stylesheet (style.css).

Web Fonts: Integrated Google Fonts.

Responsive Layout: Designed to adapt cleanly across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

Technical Justification: Native HTML5 and CSS3 were chosen without external frameworks or heavy libraries to guarantee maximum page load speed, minimal bundle size, and clean code correctness as specified in the project guidelines.
